Dental FAQ

Questions patients often ask

How do I book a dental appointment?

Call the clinic, message us on WhatsApp, or use the appointment form below. The clinic team will confirm an available appointment time.

Do I need an X-ray before treatment?

Not always. The dentist will recommend dental imaging when it is clinically useful for diagnosis or treatment planning.

How much does dental treatment cost?

Costs depend on the diagnosis, treatment type, materials and clinical complexity. Ask the clinic for a consultation and a written estimate based on your case.

Do you treat children?

The clinic provides age-appropriate dental visits and preventive guidance for children. Please confirm the exact pediatric services and age range with the clinic.

Can I get dental treatment if I am nervous?

Tell the dental team about your anxiety before treatment. The dentist can explain the procedure and discuss appropriate comfort options available at the clinic.
